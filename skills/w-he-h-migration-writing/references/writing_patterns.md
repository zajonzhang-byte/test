# Writing Patterns

Use this reference for drafting paper or thesis sections in English or Chinese.

## Abstract Pattern

One paragraph:

1. Problem: H isotope migration and retention in W are affected by He-induced defects.
2. Gap: the influence of interstitial He_n clusters on the elementary H migration barrier is not fully isolated.
3. Method: VASP DFT plus CINEB, with controlled He_n configurations and H endpoint sites.
4. Results: report only real values or trends from the user's data.
5. Mechanism: connect barrier change to local distortion, trapping, or electronic evidence.
6. Implication: relevance for H isotope retention and multiscale modeling in plasma-facing W.

## Introduction Pattern

Use 4 to 6 paragraphs:

1. Fusion context and why W is used as a plasma-facing material.
2. Why H isotope behavior matters: migration, trapping, retention, permeation, and safety.
3. Why He changes the problem: He clustering, bubbles, local strain, and defect evolution.
4. What prior work covers: H in pristine W, He in W, H-He-vacancy complexes, bubbles, MD/DFT studies.
5. Gap: direct CINEB comparison of H migration near interstitial He_n clusters.
6. Contribution: the user's controlled He_n models, stable H sites, migration paths, barriers, and mechanism.

## Methods Pattern

Separate static DFT and CINEB:

```markdown
First, all initial and final configurations were fully relaxed using DFT as implemented in VASP. The He_n clusters were constructed at interstitial positions in bcc W, and H was placed at candidate neighboring interstitial sites to identify locally stable endpoint states.

Second, H migration pathways between selected endpoint states were calculated using the climbing image nudged elastic band method. The He_n cluster was retained in the local environment so that the computed barrier reflects H migration under the strain and interaction field generated by the cluster.
```

Then insert actual cutoff, k-points, convergence, supercell, and image number.

## Results Pattern

Organize by mechanism:

1. He_n cluster structure and stability.
2. Preferred H sites near He_n.
3. H migration paths and CINEB barriers.
4. Cluster-size dependence from He_1 to He_6.
5. Mechanistic interpretation from geometry, energy, and electronic evidence.

Avoid organizing only as "Figure 1 shows..., Figure 2 shows...". Each paragraph should make a technical claim.

## Discussion Pattern

Use the discussion to answer "why":

- If He_n increases the barrier: discuss trapping, local compressive/tensile distortion, reduced pathway openness, or stronger H-He/defect interaction.
- If He_n decreases the barrier: discuss local free volume, destabilization of the initial site, or a lowered saddle configuration.
- If the trend is non-monotonic: discuss cluster geometry and path dependence rather than forcing a simple "more He means stronger effect" story.
- Connect DFT barriers to multiscale implications carefully; CINEB barriers are inputs for kinetic models, not direct retention predictions by themselves.

## Chinese Thesis Phrases

Useful section titles:

- 研究背景与意义
- 钨中氢/氦行为的研究现状
- 中间隙氦团簇对氢迁移的影响机制
- 第一性原理与爬山图像弹性带计算方法
- 结果与讨论
- 现有问题与本文工作

Useful transitions:

- 值得注意的是
- 相比之下
- 这一差异可能来源于
- 然而，现有研究仍主要集中于
- 因此，有必要在原子尺度上进一步明确
- 该结果表明，氦团簇不仅改变氢的稳定位置，也可能重塑其迁移路径
