## Ideas Adopted

- Route tasks by artifact and output type.
- Prefer direct academic output over returning generic prompts.
- Use literature matrices and paper-card style traceability.
- Synthesize by theme, mechanism, and evidence comparability.
- Distinguish author claims from assistant interpretation.
- Use strict review gates before declaring a section ready.
- Keep final claims tied to source files, notes, or explicit user-provided results.
