# Review Quality Gate

Use this reference for strict review of drafts, outlines, methods sections, and result interpretation.

## Verdict Levels

Choose one when reviewing a draft:

- Ready after minor polish: argument is coherent, numbers are sourced, methods are complete.
- Needs technical revision: core idea is sound, but method details, comparisons, or mechanism are incomplete.
- Not ready: claims are unsupported, data are missing, or the novelty is not defensible.

## Critical Checks

1. Scope: Is the work clearly about interstitial He clusters, not generic He bubbles or vacancy-He complexes?
2. Novelty: Does the draft explain what is new about H migration near He_n clusters?
3. Methods: Are VASP and CINEB settings sufficient for reproduction?
4. Energies: Are formation, binding, trapping, and migration energies defined with explicit reference states?
5. CINEB: Are endpoints, images, convergence, and forward/reverse barriers handled correctly?
6. Evidence: Does every mechanism claim have geometry, energy, or electronic evidence?
7. Literature: Are prior papers synthesized by theme and comparability rather than listed one by one?
8. Fusion relevance: Does the draft connect atomic barriers to H isotope retention cautiously?
9. Language: Are statements appropriately hedged when evidence is partial?
10. Figures and tables: Do captions state the technical takeaway, not only the plotted quantity?

## Common Major Findings

Use these as review language when applicable:

- The draft overgeneralizes vacancy-mediated H-He trapping results to interstitial He_n clusters without explaining why the defect environments are comparable.
- The CINEB barrier is reported as a single number even though the initial and final states are not energetically equivalent; forward and reverse barriers should be separated.
- The methods section lacks enough information to reproduce the calculation, especially supercell size, k-point mesh, cutoff energy, image number, and force criterion.
- The discussion claims an electronic mechanism, but the evidence shown is structural only. Either add charge/electronic analysis or soften the claim.
- The literature review is still paper-by-paper; it needs to be reorganized around H-He interaction, He clustering, defect environment, and migration-barrier evidence.
