# Review Quality Checklist

Use this checklist before finalizing an outline, section, or full literature review.

## Scope

- The review question is clear and narrow enough to answer.
- Material system, environment, property, mechanism, and application are defined.
- The review explains why the topic matters for fusion devices, not just for materials science generally.

## Evidence

- Key experimental conditions are reported when comparing studies.
- Neutron irradiation, ion irradiation, and plasma exposure are not treated as equivalent.
- Hydrogen isotope retention/permeation claims distinguish H, D, and T when needed.
- Characterization evidence is connected to the proposed mechanism.
- Modeling results are identified as modeling and are not presented as direct experimental proof.

## Synthesis

- Paragraphs begin with technical claims, not paper names.
- Similar studies are compared by conditions, material state, and mechanism.
- Conflicting results are explained rather than hidden.
- Gaps are specific and testable.
- The review explains which findings are most relevant to reactor conditions.

## Writing

- Terms are used consistently.
- Tables or figures have a clear analytical purpose.
- The conclusion does more than repeat the introduction.
- The future outlook separates near-term experimental needs from long-term engineering challenges.

## Red Flags

- Every paragraph starts with "某某等研究了".
- The review has many results but no mechanism map.
- Experimental conditions are missing.
- The text says "仍需进一步研究" without naming what should be measured or compared.
- A single paper is used to support a broad claim about all fusion materials.
